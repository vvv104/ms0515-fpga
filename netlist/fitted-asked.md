# What the photographs did not tell (asked of the owner, 2026-11-08)

The crops, each with a red frame around what is asked, are in the owner's
`photos/ask/` beside `photos/known-machines/` (outside the repository);
the source is `MC 0515 04.jpg` unless said otherwise.  `netlist/fitted.csv`
carries the answers once given.

**Answered 2026-11-08** (in `fitted.csv`): the PROM labels - left «КДРВ»
(D13), right «СТРВ» (D21); the pair with the ИР16s' mark - К555ИР16, as
on Radon17's board (so D63/D69 are the К555КП2 9008 and КР1533КП2 9106 of
the close-up, in an order the photographs do not tell); D39 has no date
(a wiped place, nothing under it); the can by D15 is the 8 MHz crystal
BQ1, «8000 / ЕГН or КГН / 4184»; D85's date 9006 or 9008.

Answered later the same day: D7 КР531ИД14 dated 9010; the two 8-pin
DIPs D20/D28 cannot be read by the owner either; BQ2's can shows one line
to the camera - «221» or «201», a wire across it, a «0» at the end.

**Still asked**, only if the photograph allows: in `3-top-strip.png`,
D48 (КР559ИП11, its date), D90 (К554СА3А - is it there, what it reads)
and VT1/VT2 (КТ677АС/КТ676АС).
