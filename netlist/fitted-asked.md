# What the photographs did not tell (asked of the owner, 2026-11-08)

The crops, each with a red frame around what is asked, are in the owner's
`photos/ask/` beside `photos/known-machines/` (outside the repository);
the source is `MC 0515 04.jpg` unless said otherwise.  `netlist/fitted.csv`
carries the answers once given.

1. **`1-prom-labels.png`** - the two КР556РТ12 under paper labels (D13
   «КДРВ», D21 «СТРВ» by ПЭ3's note).  The right one reads «СТР В»; the
   left one's writing is not legible.  Which is which, and what the left
   label says.
2. **`2-kp2-pair.png`** - two chips in the frame with the same maker's
   mark as the ИР16s, their type and date not legible.  By the rows they
   are among D49/D55 (К555ИР16) and D63/D69 (К555КП2); the museum's
   close-up shows one К555КП2 9008 and one КР1533КП2 9106 there.  Which
   position carries which.
3. **`3-top-strip.png`** - the row under the memory module's edge: D7
   (КР531ИД14, its date), the two 8-pin DIPs D20/D28 (КР1102АП15 by
   ПЭ3 - their marking), D48 (КР559ИП11 - date), D90 (К554СА3А), VT1/VT2
   (КТ677АС/КТ676АС) - are they there and what do they read.
4. **`4-lp5-date.png`** - D39 К555ЛП5: its date code.
5. **`5-crystal.png`** - the crystal by D91: its marking (the netlist
   says 15 MHz).
6. **`7-bottom-rows-right.png`** - the bottom rows' right end: the chip
   left of the lowest К555КП2 (D85, К555ЛИ3 by ПЭ3 - its marking), and
   D96 К555ИВ3 / D99 КР1533ЛН1 dates if legible.

Not asked yet, a pass of their own: the resistors' colour bands, the
capacitors' markings (the yellow К10-17 read «2F8 .22» where legible),
the diodes and the two transistor arrays.
