# The bench's parts as logic

What the C++ bench plays around the board (`sim/board/`) - the floppy drive,
the keyboard, the camera on the video DAC's inputs - becomes logic here for
the MiSTer core (plan stage 7c).  These are not chips of the board: their
ports are the board's nets on one side and MiSTer's interfaces on the
other, all on the master clock.  Each has its test in `sim/parts/` and is
checked on the board's own levels against the bench part it replaces
(`sim/board/test_board_*.cpp`).

- `mc5305.sv` (with `mc5305_bytes.sv`, `mc5305_writer.sv`,
  `mc5305_pkg.sv`): the floppy drive on XP4 - the image through MiSTer's
  block interface, the track under the head in a buffer, its bytes made as
  they pass the head as the image's formatter wrote them (ОМЕГА, vvv, FDZ)
  and put out as MFM flux pulses; what the controller writes read back
  from ДЗП's falls into sectors, each written to the image as its CRC
  checks.  Tick for tick the bench's drive (`sim/parts/test_mc5305.cpp`),
  but for the few hundred microseconds after the head moves, while the
  logic loads the track and catches up with the turn.  With no image
  mounted there is no disk in: nothing is loaded, no index, ready or flux.
- `ns4_video.sv`: the picture - the levels the camera takes at each ТС15 H
  rise, out a line later with a pixel enable, the syncs and the line and
  frame blankings apart (the board ties the two blankings on one wire).

The machine they make with the board is `rtl/ms0515.sv` (plan stage 7d):
the board as the core takes it (`rtl/board/ns4_machine.sv`, generated by
`tools/board.py`), the drive, the picture and the supply's power-on pulse;
`sim/board/test_machine.cpp` holds it against the bench's board tick for
tick.
