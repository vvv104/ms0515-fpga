# The owner's machine: its nameplate

the club-museum at the hand-over, 30.06.2015): the plate on the case.

Read by the owner and here (2026-11-03): «ЭЛЕКТРОНИКА МС 0515.08», «ЗАВОДСКОЙ №
1X7XX48», «ДАТА ИЗГОТОВЛЕНИЯ 07 1991».  The punch struck only the tops of
some figures: the three X are each 0 or 9 - not 8, whose top is much
narrower than theirs, as the plate's own 8s show; 1, 7, 4 and 8 are clear.

## Its RAM disk

`$MS0515_DATA/photos/known-machines/0_d4979_65e0bd2a_XXXL.jpg` (the solder
side) has two things written by hand:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `module-date.png` | on the solder side, at the left: «09.11.93 г.» |
| `module-label.png` | a paper label on the module's connector: «проверен по тест.» (checked by the test program) |

The machine was made in 07.1991; the module is dated 09.11.1993, more than
two years later - the owner's account: the machine went back to the plant
for it, and the plant showed the module as a "new development".  The two
non-factory changes on the board (D70.15 on GND, `АД03` to D80.5,
`docs/findings.md`) were most likely made then.  The module and the
machine are now with others; a museum in St Petersburg has been looking
for the module (the owner, 2026-11-03).
