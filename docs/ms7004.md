# The МС 7004 keyboard

The keyboard unit «Электроника МС 7004», read from its documents as the
board is (`docs/plan.md`, 7c): a netlist in `netlist/ms7004/`, its chips as
modules, the unit generated and run beside the board; inside the MiSTer core
it stands at XS2 in place of a keyboard outside, MiSTer's USB keyboard on
its matrix.

## What the documents say

- **The processor board** (Appendix 3): D1 КМ1816ВЕ48, "an analogue of
  M5L8035LP", runs the program of D5 К573РФ2 through the address latch D4
  К589ИР12 (strobed by ALE; its bits crossed on the drawing, inputs and
  outputs alike, so the address comes out straight): P20..P22 are A8..A10,
  P23 drives CE, PSEN drives OE.  The crystal Z1 is 4.608 MHz (PSEN 3.2 us,
  Fig. 3).  D8 КР580ВР43 (an 8243, on P20..P23 and PROG) puts the scan's
  running one on its sixteen outputs, 13 us each, the matrix scanned in
  3.5 ms (Fig. 3).
- **A key** is a capacitive sensor between a scan line ВР and a return
  line ГР: a pulse on ВР comes through a pressed key strongly enough to pass
  the threshold.  D10 564КП2 (a CD4051) picks one ГР by P10..P12; VT1 and
  the comparator D9 К554СА3 (open collector, R14 to +5 V) make it a logic
  pulse, which clocks D11 К155ТМ2 (D and S tied high through R23) set; its Q
  is T1.  The firmware clears it through P14 and the inverter D2.1.
- **The line**: the firmware sends bit by bit on P17 through D7 К1102АП15
  (a bipolar 8-12 V swing), and takes the computer's bytes on INT through
  D6 К1102ЛП1.  The cable has four wires (XT1: ground, +12 V, in, out).
- **Indicators and bell**: LEDs on P24..P27 through D3 К155ЛН3; the bell
  BA1 on P13 through D2.3, a click or about 2 kHz.
- **Power**: +12 V in; D12 КР142ЕН5В makes +5 V, a converter on D2 and
  VT2..VT5 driven by PSEN makes -12 V.

## The matrix

`netlist/ms7004/matrix.csv`: each key's scan and return line, the legend
drawn, and the firmware's code for its place - the drawing's column k is
ВРk, its row m is ГРm.  The two sources agree for every key with a legend
but where noted:

- SA73 is drawn «Ъ» and SA79 «~», but the firmware has 375 (Table 1's
  «~») at SA73's place and 304 («Ъ») at SA79's.  The drawing's matrix rows
  are not the keyboard's rows, so Fig. 1 does not settle which key cap is
  which; the codes are the firmware's either way.
- SA91, with no legend, has ИСП's code 175 as SA99 does: likely the second
  sensor of a double-width key.
- SA17, drawn by ВР and КМП, has its column line routed along the board's
  edge to column 11 (the high-resolution scan shows it): it shares ВР11 and
  ГР8 with SA82, a place the firmware has no code for.
- Table 1 prints «, <» with 365 (the firmware's is 363) and Table 2 the
  left arrow as «→» 247: misprints, the firmware and the other entries
  agree.
