# The МС 7004 keyboard

The keyboard unit «Электроника МС 7004», read from its documents as the
board is (`docs/plan.md`, 7c): a netlist in `netlist/ms7004/`, its chips as
modules, the unit generated and run beside the board; inside the MiSTer core
it stands at XS2 in place of a keyboard outside, MiSTer's USB keyboard on
its matrix.

## What the documents say

- **The processor board** (Appendix 3): D1 КМ1816ВЕ48, "an analogue of
  M5L8035LP", runs the program of D5 К573РФ2 through the address latch D4
  К589ИР12 (strobed by ALE; its bits crossed on the drawing, inputs and
  outputs alike, so the address comes out straight): P20..P22 are A8..A10,
  P23 drives CE, PSEN drives OE.  The crystal Z1 is 4.608 MHz (PSEN 3.2 us,
  Fig. 3).  D8 КР580ВР43 (an 8243, on P20..P23 and PROG) puts the scan's
  running one on its sixteen outputs, 13 us each, the matrix scanned in
  3.5 ms (Fig. 3).
- **A key** is a capacitive sensor between a scan line ВР and a return
  line ГР: a pulse on ВР comes through a pressed key strongly enough to pass
  the threshold.  D10 564КП2 (a CD4051) picks one ГР by P10..P12; VT1 and
  the comparator D9 К554СА3 (open collector, R14 to +5 V) make it a logic
  pulse, which clocks D11 К155ТМ2 (D and S tied high through R23) set; its Q
  is T1.  The firmware clears it through P14 and the inverter D2.1.
- **The line**: the firmware sends bit by bit on P17 through D7 К1102АП15
  (a bipolar 8-12 V swing), and takes the computer's bytes on INT through
  D6 К1102ЛП1.  The cable has four wires (XT1: ground, +12 V, in, out).
- **Indicators and bell**: LEDs on P24..P27 through D3 К155ЛН3; the bell
  BA1 on P13 through D2.3, a click or about 2 kHz.
- **Power**: +12 V in; D12 КР142ЕН5В makes +5 V, a converter on D2 and
  VT2..VT5 driven by PSEN makes -12 V.

## The matrix

`netlist/ms7004/matrix.csv`: each key's scan and return line, the legend
drawn, and the firmware's code for its place - the drawing's column k is
ВРk, its row m is ГРm.  The two sources agree for every key with a legend
but where noted:

- SA73 is drawn «Ъ» and SA79 «~», but the firmware has 375 (Table 1's
  «~») at SA73's place and 304 («Ъ») at SA79's.  The drawing's matrix rows
  are not the keyboard's rows, so Fig. 1 does not settle which key cap is
  which; the codes are the firmware's either way.
- SA91, with no legend, has ИСП's code 175 as SA99 does: likely the second
  sensor of a double-width key.
- SA17, drawn by ВР and КМП, has its column line routed along the board's
  edge to column 11 (the high-resolution scan shows it): it shares ВР11 and
  ГР8 with SA82, a place the firmware has no code for.
- Table 1 prints «, <» with 365 (the firmware's is 363) and Table 2 the
  left arrow as «→» 247: misprints, the firmware and the other entries
  agree.

## The keyboard from its netlist

`tools/board.py` writes `rtl/board/ms7004.sv` from `netlist/ms7004/` beside
the НС4 board: its chips as modules - the 8035 (`km1816ve48.sv`,
`docs/km1816ve48.md`), the 8243, the 8212, the 2716 with the firmware, the
small TTL - the 4.608 MHz crystal from a phase accumulator
(`crystal_frac.sv`: 24 cycles in 625 master ticks), the threshold
amplifier as its treatment says, the pads to the matrix board and XT1
brought out.  Its chips have no delay lines yet: its fastest edges, the
8035's, come a crystal half-period (108 ns) apart.

`sim/board/test_ms7004.cpp` runs it on its own firmware from power-on,
the bench pressing keys on the pads:

- port 2's four indicators lit while it beeps twice at 2 kHz, then all
  dark (the listing's start; ТО 5.4, Table 3's footnote); ЛАТ, on P15, is
  set by the beep's OUTL P1 from RAM not yet cleared - on the board as the
  RAM came up, off in the model, whose RAM starts at zero;
- every ВР line a pulse of 8..18 us (Fig. 3: 13 us);
- А/A pressed (SA34 on ВР5/ГР5): 322 (Table 1);
- the computer's 253 (AB, ROM-B's question): 001 000 (Table 3).

`probe_ms7004` (build tree) prints where the 8035 goes from power-on.

## In the core: a PC keyboard on its matrix

`rtl/board/ms7004_machine.sv` is the keyboard as the core takes it (its
line, its reset, the matrix's lines, the indicators, the bell);
`rtl/ms0515.sv` puts it at XS2, its reset the board's supply.  The matrix
board is `rtl/parts/ms7004_matrix.sv`: a pressed key puts its scan
line's pulse on its return line.  `rtl/parts/ms7004_keys.sv` presses the
keys from MiSTer's `ps2_key`, its tables made by `tools/ms7004_keys.py`
from `netlist/ms7004/pc_keys.csv`:

- by position: a PC key is the МС 7004 key where it stands (ЙЦУКЕН),
  Shift and all;
- by character: the key with the PC cap's character, its ВР pressed or
  let go as the character needs, РУС/ЛАТ turned over and back for one the
  mode at hand lacks - the mode read off the keyboard's ЛАТ indicator,
  which the computer sets.  Each step holds 20 ms, two scans and the
  bounce check; keys typed meanwhile wait.

The character mapping is the emulator's (`src/frontend/src/Keymap.cpp`
there), its keys named here by their legends; its codes are not taken:
the firmware's are.  The two differ where the emulator's own table does:
it gives 5 the code 325 (the firmware and Table 1: 326) and Ъ the code 361
of «_» (the firmware: 375 on the key drawn Ъ) - findings for the emulator.
The PC keys the emulator leaves free carry the core's choices: Esc as Ф11
(АР2, the DEC terminals' Esc), NumLock, keypad /, * and ScrollLock as
ПФ1..ПФ4, keypad - and + as the keypad's minus and comma, PrintScreen as
ПМ, Menu as ИСП; Ф13, Ф14 and Ф17..Ф20 have no PC key yet.
