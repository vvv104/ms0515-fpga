# КМ1816ВЕ48 - the МС 7004's processor

The keyboard's D1 is a КМ1816ВЕ48 working as an 8035 (EA high: every
fetch from outside, through the latch D4 and the EPROM D5; ТО 5.1).  The
model is built from Intel's documents only:

| What | Where (`$MS0515_DATA/docs/mcs48/`) |
|---|---|
| MCS-48 Family User's Manual, 1980 (bitsavers, `components/intel/8048/1980_MCS-48_Users_Manual.pdf`, SHA1 46f6073c5edea1e53755b96009fd73f5779259ce) | the instruction set (chapter 4), the machine cycle (2.1.11, "MCS-48 cycle timing", p. 2-10), what each instruction does in each state ("Instruction timing diagram", p. 2-11), the expanded system (3.1, 3.3, 3.6, 3.7) |
| The 8048H/8035HL data sheet, in the same manual (pp. 6-5, 6-6) | "Bus timing as a function of tCY", the waveforms of a fetch from external program memory and of port 2 with the 8243 |

## The machine cycle

The crystal is divided by 3 into states and the states by 5 into a
machine cycle (2.1.11): a cycle is 15 crystal periods, 30 half-periods.
The data sheet's bus timing, in fractions of tCY, puts every edge on a
half-period:

| Symbol | Fraction of tCY | Half-periods |
|---|---|---|
| tLL, ALE high | 7/30 | 7 |
| tAL, address before ALE falls | 1/10 | 3 |
| tLA, address after ALE falls | 1/15 | 2 |
| tAFC, address floats before PSEN falls | 1/30 | 1 |
| tCC(2), PSEN low | 2/5 | 12 |
| tRD(2), PSEN low to data in (max) | 3/10 | 9 |
| tCA, PSEN high to ALE high | 1/15 | 2 (min) |

They are approximate and without gate delays ("approximate values not
including gate delays"), minimums where so marked; the model takes them as
they are, the states as the cycle figure draws them: ALE high across S4,
PSEN low from S5 into S2 - the fetch of the next cycle's instruction
("Instruction cycle": output address in S5, input instruction in S1).

The pins, in half-periods h of a cycle - S1 h 0..5, S2 6..11, S3 12..17,
S4 18..23, S5 24..29 - every edge on a half-period of the crystal:

| Pin | When | From |
|---|---|---|
| ALE high | h 17..23 | tLL 7; high across S4 (cycle figure) |
| BUS: the fetch address | h 21..25 | tAL 3 before ALE falls, tLA 2 after |
| PSEN low | h 27 .. h 8 of the next cycle | tAFC 1 after the address floats, tCC(2) 12 |
| BUS: the byte taken | as PSEN rises (h 9) | tRD(2) 9: valid from h 6 |
| ALE high again | h 17 | tCA 8 >= 2 |
| PROG low (MOVD, ANLD, ORLD) | h 24 of cycle 1 .. h 8 of cycle 2 | tPP >= 0.48 tCY (1200 ns at 6 MHz): 15; read P2 lower in cycle 2 S2 (p. 2-11) |
| P20..P23: the port's data | h 9 .. h 18 | "I/O information is always present on Port 2 lower at the rising edge of ALE" (3.1.3); tPL before, tLP after it |
| P20..P23: the fetch's PC11..PC8 | h 19 .. h 8 of the next cycle | stable while PSEN is low (3.1.4) |
| P20..P23: the 8243's order (cycle 1 of MOVD, ANLD, ORLD) | h 12 .. h 25 | "output opcode/address" in S3 (p. 2-11), tCP and tPC around PROG's fall |
| P20..P23: the data written, or off for a read | h 26 .. h 9 of cycle 2 | "output data to P2 lower" in S5 (p. 2-11), tDP and tPD around PROG's rise |

A fetch slot - the address in S4, PSEN across S5 and S1 - brings the byte
the next cycle takes in its S1: the next instruction, or an instruction's
immediate data or jump target in its second cycle; a cycle 2 with neither
(IN, OUTL, MOVD, ...) has no PSEN, its slot free for PROG.
